Developer & Security Information

Cryptographic Architecture

  • Derivation: PBKDF2 with SHA-256 (100,000 rounds)
  • Salts: 16-byte cryptographically secure random salts
  • Lock Scope: Session-scoped (purged on Chrome exit)
  • Fail Model: Fail-Closed (defaults to LOCKED)
  • Network: 100% Offline (zero analytics, zero external requests)
